# Pods and Packs

## Pods

Fold a handful of organisms into a pod - select them, right-click, Make Pod. One name on the canvas, the whole garden alive inside it. It breathes through PodIn and PodOut, in audio, MIDI or video.

## Packs

Organisms arrive in packs.

- **Core** mixing, routing, shaping.
- **Humus** instruments, effects, drums and bass.
- **AV** decks, mixers and scenes.

Create your own packs and share it with the world!
