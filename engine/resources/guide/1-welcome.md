# Welcome

## Welcome to Humus Beta!

Thank you for taking part in testing Humus! You are one of the first people to arrive here, please make yourself at home!

Anything you try, break, dislike or wish for is worth hearing about.

## What's Humus?

Humus is "the fertile layer of the soil formed by decomposing organic matter" which joins all forms of life.

A patch here works in a similar way: audio and video instruments feeding each other, interacting together.
