# Plant and Cords

## Organisms

Double-click an empty spot on the canvas and pick one: an instrument, an effect, a mixer, or a video deck. The picker searches as you type, and right-click gives the same thing beside the rest of the canvas menu.

Select an organism and its knobs will appear in the Properties panel on the right, map them to a controller, automate them, or roll a dice on them to discover new random parameters.

## Cords

- **Audio** solid, out of the bottom of one organism into the top of the next.
- **MIDI** dashed, for notes, clocks and controllers.
- **Video** frames on their way to a screen.

The edges of the world are organisms too: SoundIn and SoundOut are your audio interface, MidiIn your keyboard, VideoOut a window or a projector.
