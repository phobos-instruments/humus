# Tremolo

A stereo amplitude modulator: an LFO sweeps the volume at Rate Hz, Depth deep.

## Parameters

**Rate** LFO speed in Hz (0.1.. 20).

**Depth** How deep the volume dips (0 = off, 1 = full silence at the trough).

**Waveform** LFO shape: Sine, Triangle or Square (choppy gating).

**Sync** Lock one LFO cycle to an eighth note of the transport tempo instead of the free Rate.
