# Send

The tap: split a signal toward an effect without re-plumbing the patch. The stereo input passes through outlets 1-2 completely untouched, and a copy leaves through outlets 3-4 at the Send Level - cord that pair into an echo, a Bus, a spare SoundOut, anything that should hear the signal too.

## Inlets

**1-2** The signal. A cord into only inlet 1 bridges to both sides.

## Outlets

**1-2** Thru - bit-identical to the input, always.

**3-4** The send - the same signal scaled by Send Level.

## Send Level

The strength of the copy. 1 sends at unity; 0 mutes the send; above 1 drives the effect harder. The thru pair never changes.

Bypass mutes the send and keeps the thru pair - killing a send never interrupts the dry signal.

## Related Organisms

Bus, Mixer, Gain
